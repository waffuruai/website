## Analytics and Global Privacy Control

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages help people. Google Analytics truncates IP addresses and does not store the full address.

- Where the law requires your consent, which includes the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Quebec, analytics cookies stay off until you accept them.
- Elsewhere we show a notice, and you can opt out at any time.
- Analytics runs under Google Consent Mode v2, so the choice you make is passed to Google.
- If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out and analytics stays off, wherever you are.

You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer. The [Cookie policy](/legal/cookies) lists every cookie. We do not use analytics data for advertising.

## Our software and your data

Our products are local-first. Data that our software processes on your machine, such as your prompts, notes, files and model output, stays on that machine unless you choose to turn on a feature that sends it elsewhere. We will tell you plainly when a feature does that, and what it sends. This policy covers the Site and our communications. If a product starts to send data off your device, it will come with its own notice.

## How we use information

We use personal information to:

- send mailing-list updates to people who asked for them;
- review early-access requests, choose who to invite and stay in touch with participants;
- answer your emails;
- run, secure and improve the Site;
- meet legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## Legal bases

If you are in the EU, EEA or United Kingdom, we rely on these bases:

- **Consent** for the mailing list and for analytics cookies. You can withdraw it at any time, which does not affect what we did before.
- **Steps at your request before a contract**, and our **legitimate interest** in running the early-access program, for early-access requests.
- **Legitimate interests** in running and securing the Site, understanding how it is used where consent is not required, and answering the people who write to us. We weigh these against your rights and stop if yours prevail.
- **Legal obligation** where we have to keep or disclose information by law.

## Who we share it with

We share personal information only with:

- **Google**, for Google Forms and its response sheets, and for Google Analytics if you have allowed it;
- **Cloudflare**, for hosting, delivery and security;
- professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, under confidentiality;
- authorities, when the law requires it;
- a successor, if Waffuru is merged with or sold to another company, with notice to you.

These providers process information on our behalf and under their own data-processing terms. **We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.**

## International transfers

We are based in North America, and our providers run services around the world. Your information can be processed in the United States, Canada and other countries where they operate. When we transfer information from the EEA or the United Kingdom, we rely on safeguards such as adequacy decisions, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for providers that are certified, and standard contractual clauses.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time, at [hello@waffuru.ai](mailto:hello@waffuru.ai?subject=Privacy), to:

- tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- stop or limit how we use it;
- move it to another service;
- stop sending you mailing-list email. Every update will say how, and you can always write to us. To unsubscribe now, ask us and we remove you.

Which rights apply depends on where you live.

- **EU, EEA and United Kingdom (GDPR, UK GDPR).** Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdrawal of consent. You can complain to your data-protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK.
- **California (CCPA and CPRA).** Know and access what we collect, delete it, correct it, opt out of sale or sharing of personal information, and not be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of, and we honour Global Privacy Control signals for analytics. An authorized agent can make a request for you.
- **Canada (PIPEDA and provincial laws).** Access your information, challenge its accuracy, withdraw consent, and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial regulator.
- **Elsewhere.** We will honour similar rights as far as your local law requires.

We may need to verify who you are before acting. We reply within the time the law sets, which is usually one month (GDPR) or 45 days (California).
