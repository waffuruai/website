This policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage that waffuru.ai (the **Site**) uses. It goes with our [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy), which explains what we do with personal information more broadly.

## What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Browsers also offer local storage, which works in a similar way. Both let a site remember something between pages and visits. We call them all "cookies" below unless the difference matters.

## Necessary storage

The first three rows are always on. They keep the Site working the way you set it up, and they do not track you or go to anyone else. You can delete them in your browser settings, and the Site will then forget your theme, language and cookie choice.

## Analytics cookies

The `_ga` cookies belong to Google Analytics 4, which we use to see which pages are useful and to improve the Site. They carry a random identifier, not your name or email address. Google Analytics truncates IP addresses and does not store the full address. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone but Google, our analytics processor.

Whether they are on depends on where you are:

- **EU, EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland and Quebec.** Analytics cookies stay off until you accept them.
- **Elsewhere.** We show a notice, analytics can run, and you can opt out at any time.

Analytics runs under Google Consent Mode v2: the choice you make is passed to Google, and with analytics declined Google Analytics does not set its cookies.

## Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a [Global Privacy Control](https://globalprivacycontrol.org) signal, we treat it as an opt-out: analytics stays off wherever you are, and we tell you so in the cookie notice.

## Change your choice

Choose **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page at any time to accept or decline analytics. Declining stops analytics from then on. To remove cookies that are already set, delete them in your browser settings. Most browsers also let you block cookies altogether, though some parts of the Site may then not remember your settings.

## Changes

We may update this policy, for example if we add a new cookie. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when. If analytics or any non-essential cookie changes in a way that needs your consent, we will ask again.
