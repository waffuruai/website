The stack.

Iron compiles the kernels. Butter runs models on them. Waffle watches the GPUs. Syrup remembers what you were working on.

04 · MEMORY

Syrup

Your AI remembers what you were working on.

Local retrieval over your notes and code. Embeddings are compressed with TurboQuant+, search is lexical plus vector, and results come back with citations over MCP.

Private
03 · CONTROL PLANE

Waffle

See every GPU and every engine running on it.

A control plane for LLMs on GPUs, from one box under a desk to a neocloud renting out a fleet. Each host runs a small Rust sidecar that reports signed telemetry. Works with Butter, vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp and other OpenAI-compatible servers.

Private
02 · SERVING

Butter

Model serving without Python.

Swift on Metal for Apple silicon, Rust for CUDA and the rest. Loads 3- to 8-bit quantized checkpoints, hybrid and MoE models, with a quantized KV cache.

Private
01 · KERNELS

Iron

One kernel definition, four backends.

A Rust-embedded DSL. Kernels lower to a shared IR, then to Metal, CUDA, HIP/ROCm or Vulkan SPIR-V. A versioned C and Rust ABI lets existing hosts call them.

Private

Models on the menu.

Loaded straight from Hugging Face. Coverage is widest on Metal; CUDA support is added model by model.
